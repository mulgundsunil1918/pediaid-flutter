Generated by `python3 tools/build.py`; do not edit by hand.  The rule that governed everything: **word-for-word, nothing added, dropped or paraphrased.**  Where that left a choice about *structure* (which string a line belongs to) rather than *words*, the choice is listed here.

## 1. Instruction changes

* **`iconRow` is empty (`''`) for every entry.**  After seeing the app render the reference entry, the request was to drop the icon captions (“those icon queries you remove”).  The brief itself asks for the raw row, so this departs from it on purpose.  Nothing else about the icons is read or interpreted (the legend on p. 814 was not looked up).  It is one switch: `python3 tools/build.py --icons raw` writes the five printed captions of every box as `B/C · 1 · Yes · Yes · No`.  If what was meant was only the `?` captions, tell me and I will change it.

## 2. Structure conventions (editorial — the book prints indentation, the schema wants lists)

* `name`, `brandNames`, `drugClass` come from the gray box.  `brandNames` paragraphs are joined with `\n`; the five icon captions are the only part of the box not emitted.
* `formulations`: one string per printed formulation paragraph.  Wrapped lines are joined with one space; after a line-final `-`, `–` or `/` they are joined with no space (`mg/` + `kg` → `mg/kg`).
* `doseSections`: every level-0 bold item of the dosing block starts a `DoseSection` (heading = its leading bold text; any regular text after it on the same paragraph is that section's first line).  Deeper bold-only lines are `DoseLine(isHeading: true)`; everything else is a `DoseLine`.
* A `DoseSection` holds its lines and then at most one table, so whatever the book prints *after* a table (including its footnotes) starts a new section with `heading: ''`.
* Tables the book prints inside the formulations block come first in `doseSections`; tables inside the remarks block come last (the schema has no other slot for them).
* `remarks`: one string per printed paragraph.  Indented lists under a lead-in line (e.g. AMINOPHYLLINE “Recommended guidelines for obtaining levels:” + its items) are one string per printed item.
* `pregnancyNote`: the paragraph(s) that begin “Pregnancy category …” / “Pregnancy code …”, moved out of `remarks` as the reference entry does; `''` when the book has none.  Pregnancy statements that sit inside other paragraphs (e.g. “Potassium citrate has a pregnancy category of “C” …”) stay in `remarks`.
* Cross-reference entries (“ACTH — See Corticotropin”, 45 of them) are kept as entries; the “See …” text is their only `remarks` string.
* `sourcePages` = “Harriet Lane Handbook, 24th ed. — pp. N–M” using the **printed** page numbers (PDF page + 809, checked against the running header of every page).

## 3. What was removed as page furniture (and only that)

Removed by position/shape, never by matching drug text: the top header band (`Chapter 31 <letter>`, printed page number, `Part IV Formulary`), the right-hand sidebar (`For explanation of icons, see p. 814`, `FORMULARY`), the `Continued` line at the foot of a page, the `NAME continued` header at the top of a continuation page (it may wrap onto two lines and may contain subscripts), the large letter bands, the five icon captions inside each gray box, and the icon pictures.  Table captions, footnotes and the 6-pt abbreviation lines under tables are content and are kept.

## 7. Characters and glyphs

* En dashes (U+2013) and every other typographic character are kept as printed: curly quotes ‘ ’ “ ”, ≥ ≤ ÷ × ± ∼ − (U+2212), — (U+2014), ― (U+2015, in names like “ARGININE HYDROCHLORIDE―INJECTABLE PREPARATION”), ½ ¼ ¾ ⅓, ®, é è ï ö.  Nothing is normalised.
* Subscripts and superscripts are written as the real Unicode characters (`T₁/₂`, `m²`, `PO₄`, `Ca²⁺`, `ᵃ` footnote markers).  Where no Unicode form exists the plain character is kept: a superscript comma (`Pertzyeᵃ,ᶜ`, p. 343) and the braces in `AUC{₂₄}` (p. 458, printed with literal braces).
* Glyphs the text layer encodes as a different letter were read from the page: a Greek alpha set in ESSTIX-Twelve (`a` in the layer, `α` printed) in PHENTOLAMINE (p. 354); the subscript capital `O` of “RHₒ(D)” is written `ₒ`; the ring of `°C`/`°F` (U+25E6 in the layer) is written `°`; a superscript letter o used as a degree sign (AZTREONAM p. 72, “2–8ᵒ C”) is written `ᵒ`, as printed.
* The fraction slash in ARGININE (p. 60, “2⁄3”, U+2044) is printed tight against its digits; the blanks the text layer puts around it were removed.
* Typos and oddities in the book are kept: “Anacin Asprin Free” (ACETAMINOPHEN), “Tricitrates1ᵃ” (CITRATE MIXTURES), “NaÏve” (DEXMETHYLPHENIDATE p. 147).
* A table or paragraph that wraps after `/`, `-` or `–` is joined without a space.  A soft hyphen (a hyphen added by the typesetter, not part of the word) is dropped only when the joined word exists elsewhere in the book and the hyphenated form does not; 2 such joins:
  * PDF p. 56: `Pharmacoki-` + `netics` → `Pharmacokinetics`
  * PDF p. 426: `post-` + `dose` → `postdose`

## 10. Limits

* The visual check of non-table text was a sample (paragraph grouping), not every line; the completeness check (§5) covers every character of every entry.
* Where the book's hanging indent is inconsistent (a few paragraphs have their last lines indented one more step, e.g. BENZTROPINE p. 76), text continuity wins over indentation.
* Cell text of tables whose cells wrap very narrowly can differ from the printed line breaks (spaces vs `\n`), never in words.
* The generated files import `../reference/drug_entry_v3.dart`; adjust the import when the files move into the app repo.
